# Master's Thesis: Research on Beamforming Methods for Multi-Carrier Frequency MIMO High-Frequency Radars [Attached MATLAB Code]

1. On the basis of array signal model, the basic principles of multi-carrier signals are expounded, and a multi-carrier array signal model is established. The generation mechanism of virtual arrays under multi-frequency systems is analyzed in detail, leading to the derivation of conventional beamforming algorithms for multi-carrier MIMO radar. Experimental simulations discuss the impact of different frequency combinations on the performance of multi-carrier signal beamforming.
2. To address the issue of optimal frequency combination determination, a particle swarm algorithm is proposed for selecting the optimal carrier frequencies under a fixed virtual aperture. This approach ensures the main lobe resolution remains unchanged while selecting the carrier frequencies that yield the best sidelobe performance; when the frequency combination elements are integer multiples of the reference frequency, an exhaustive search method is utilized to select sparse arrays with optimal sidelobe performance from a virtual linear array. This model also determines the layout of the receiving array and the allocation of received signals among its elements, achieving optimal sidelobe performance while reducing the complexity of the receiving system. Finally, to address the issue of high sidelobe levels in conventional beamforming when frequency intervals are large, convex optimization theory is employed to obtain optimal weights, optimizing sidelobe performance while maintaining the main lobe resolution.
\t3,  The effect of the amplitude and phase characteristics of different frequency signals in echoes on beamforming performance is analyzed. The necessity of robust beamforming is elucidated. The Capon beamforming algorithm and its properties are analyzed in detail. The principles of the robust Capon algorithm and the robust Capon algorithm with dual constraints are elucidated. Through the simulation experiment, Under the condition of multi-carrier signals, The robust Capon algorithm has a good output signal-to-noise ratio when the input signal-to-noise ratio is low. And is insensitive to angle errors and sampling rate. Possesses certain stability.Finally, to address the unknown errors and dense interference issues, an extended RAB-SDP-WC algorithm is proposed. This robust algorithm ensures that the gain in the specified azimuth area does not degrade under worst-case conditions, minimizing the output power at its worst. It can form a depression in the dense interference region while maintaining good sidelobe performance. Simulation results show that compared to the R-SOCP algorithm, the proposed algorithm has better interference suppression capabilities and sidelobe performance.
Keywords: Multi-carrier MIMO radar; Received beamforming; Virtual array; Sidelobe suppression
The sidelobe suppression method based on convex optimization theory has gained significant attention in recent years, becoming a hot research topic. This optimization method is applied in many engineering fields such as control systems, mechanical engineering, signal processing, etc. In this section, the convex optimization theory is applied to the directional pattern synthesis problem of multi-carrier radar to reduce the sidelobe level of the beamforming directional pattern. It aims to keep the main lobe resolution unchanged while minimizing the sidelobe level of the directional pattern.
